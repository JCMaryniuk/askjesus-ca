ABOUT ASKJESUS.CA

Scripture first.

ASKJesus.ca helps you locate Bible passages relevant to the questions, struggles, decisions, and situations you are facing.

Artificial intelligence is used only to help identify relevant Scripture references. The Bible text itself is retrieved separately so you can examine God's Word directly.

The goal is simple: point you to Scripture, encourage you to read it in context, and leave room for prayer, reflection, and the leading of the Holy Spirit.
