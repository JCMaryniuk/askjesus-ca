ABOUT ASKJESUS.CA

Built to point you back to Scripture.

ASKJesus.ca is a Scripture-finding tool created to help people bring their questions, struggles, and decisions to God's Word.

Artificial intelligence is used to help identify relevant Bible passages. It does not replace prayer, the Holy Spirit, Scripture itself, or wise pastoral and professional care.

Our goal is simple: help you find Scripture, read it in context, pray, and seek God for yourself.
